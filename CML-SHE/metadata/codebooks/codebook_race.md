Race/ethnicity coding
=====================

Follows IPEDS 2010 standard. Eight categories:

  White                  non-Hispanic, self-report
  Black                  non-Hispanic
  Hispanic               any race, Hispanic origin
  Asian                  includes South Asian
  Multi                  two or more, non-Hispanic
  AIAN                   American Indian / Alaska Native
  NHPI                   Native Hawaiian / Pacific Islander
  Unknown                declined to respond or missing

Students reporting multiple non-Hispanic categories without Hispanic
origin are coded "Multi" (IPEDS bridging procedure).
