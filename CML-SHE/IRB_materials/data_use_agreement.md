# Data Use Agreement (summary)

Parties: Office of Institutional Research (provider), Principal
Investigator (receiver).

Provider grants access to de-identified administrative records for the
2013-2018 entering-freshman cohorts. Data includes admissions,
financial-aid, transcript, enrollment, and degree conferral records.

Receiver agrees to:

  1. De-identify all records prior to analysis; retain no PII.
  2. Store data on institutional research-compute infrastructure;
     no copies on personal devices.
  3. Publish only aggregated results; no cell sizes < 10.
  4. Destroy raw data files upon study completion or 2027-11-13,
     whichever comes first.
  5. Acknowledge the Office of Institutional Research in publications.

Signed [redacted], November 2022.
