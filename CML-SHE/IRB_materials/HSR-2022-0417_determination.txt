INSTITUTIONAL REVIEW BOARD - DETERMINATION LETTER

Protocol #:       HSR-2022-0417
Title:            Causal Machine Learning for Need-Based Financial Aid
                  in Sustainable Higher Education
PI:               [Redacted]
Date:             14 November 2022
Decision:         EXEMPT, category 4 (secondary research on identifiable
                  private information without contact)

Rationale
---------
The study uses administrative records that are de-identified prior to
release to the research team. No direct interaction with human
participants. Under 45 CFR 46.104(d)(4)(ii), this qualifies as exempt.

Conditions
----------
1. The research team shall retain de-identified data on encrypted,
   access-controlled storage for no more than 7 years.
2. Any attempt at re-identification (including linking to external
   datasets beyond the approved list) requires re-review.
3. An annual continuing review is not required, but the PI shall file
   a final report at study completion.

This determination is valid until 13 November 2027.
