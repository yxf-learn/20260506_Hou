Baseline intake protocol
========================

v4 (2023-08-19)

1. Window: weeks -2 to +4 around academic year start.
2. Instruments: PHQ-9, GAD-7, MSPSS, PSS-10, sleep diary (7-day).
3. Modality consent split: survey-only / survey+admin / full-multimodal.
4. Re-consent every 12 months; revocation honored within 14 days.
